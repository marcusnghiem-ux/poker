♠ Royal Texas Hold'em Guide & Rules ♠

1
Royal Flush: A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠

The unbeatable hand. Ace-high straight flush of the same suit.

2
Straight Flush: 9♥ 8♥ 7♥ 6♥ 5♥

Five consecutive cards of identical suit.

3
Four of a Kind: K♠ K♥ K♦ K♣ 4♦

Four cards of matching rank plus highest kicker.

4
Full House: J♠ J♥ J♦ 8♣ 8♠

Three cards of one rank combined with a pair of another rank.

5
Flush: A♦ J♦ 9♦ 6♦ 3♦

Any five cards of the same suit, not in sequence.

6
Straight: 10♠ 9♥ 8♦ 7♣ 6♠

Five consecutive ranks of mixed suits. Ace can count high or low (A-2-3-4-5).

7
Three of a Kind: 7♠ 7♥ 7♦ K♣ 2♠

Three cards of matching rank with two unmatched kickers.

8
Two Pair: Q♠ Q♥ 9♦ 9♣ A♠

Two distinct pairs with one kicker card.

9
One Pair: 10♥ 10♦ A♠ 8♣ 4♥

Two cards of identical rank.

10
High Card: A♠ J♥ 8♦ 6♣ 2♦

No pair or sequence. Evaluated by highest single card down to fifth card.

1. The Dealer Button & Blinds

The Dealer Button (D) rotates clockwise each hand. The player immediately left of the dealer posts the Small Blind, and the next player posts the Big Blind. These forced bets spark the action.

2. Pre-Flop

Each player receives 2 private hole cards. Betting begins with the player to the left of the Big Blind. Players may Fold, Call the big blind, or Raise.

3. The Flop

The dealer reveals 3 community cards face-up on the board. A second betting round commences starting with the first active player left of the button.

4. The Turn

A 4th community card is dealt face-up. Players evaluate their improving hands and draws before the third betting round.

5. The River & Showdown

The 5th and final community card is dealt. The final betting round occurs. If two or more players remain, they reveal their hole cards in the Showdown. The best 5-card combination wins the pot!

When players go all-in with unequal chip stacks, the engine automatically splits the betting pool into a Main Pot and one or more Side Pots. All-in players only contest pots they contributed chips to, ensuring authentic tournament rules.
