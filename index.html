Casino & Poker Official Game Rules Guide

♠ Solar Texas Hold'em Official Rules

The undisputed king of poker games. Each player receives 2 private hole cards, followed by 5 community board cards dealt across 4 betting rounds.

Poker Hand Rankings Hierarchy (Highest to Lowest)

1
Royal Flush: A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠

The unbeatable hand. Ace-high straight flush of identical suit.

2
Straight Flush: 9♥ 8♥ 7♥ 6♥ 5♥

Five consecutive cards of identical suit.

3
Four of a Kind: K♠ K♥ K♦ K♣ 4♦

Four cards of matching rank plus highest fifth kicker.

4
Full House: J♠ J♥ J♦ 8♣ 8♠

Three cards of one rank combined with a pair of another rank.

5
Flush: A♦ J♦ 9♦ 6♦ 3♦

Five cards of identical suit not in numerical sequence.

6
Straight: 10♠ 9♦ 8♥ 7♣ 6♠

Five cards of sequential rank regardless of suit.

7
Three of a Kind: Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ 9♣ 5♠

Three cards of identical rank with two unrelated kickers.

8
Two Pair: 10♠ 10♦ 7♣ 7♠ A♥

Two distinct pairs of matching ranks plus one kicker.

9
One Pair: A♠ A♦ K♣ 9♥ 4♠

Two cards of identical rank plus three kickers.

10
High Card: A♠ Q♦ 9♣ 7♥ 2♠

No pair or sequence; hand value determined by highest card.

Betting Rounds & Game Flow

  1. Blinds Posting: Small Blind and Big Blind are posted by players left of the dealer button.
  2. Pre-Flop: 2 hole cards dealt face down to each player. Betting begins with Under-The-Gun (UTG).
  3. The Flop: Dealer reveals 3 community cards face up. Second betting round begins.
  4. The Turn: A fourth community card is dealt face up. Third betting round begins.
  5. The River: The fifth and final community card is dealt. Final betting round.
  6. Showdown: Remaining players reveal hole cards. Best 5-card hand wins the pot!

♦ Short Deck (6-Plus) Hold'em Rules

Action-packed high-stakes variant popularized in Macau. All cards deuce through five (2, 3, 4, 5) are removed from the deck, leaving a 36-card deck.

Critical Rule Changes from Classic Hold'em

  • Flush Beats Full House: With only 9 cards per suit remaining, flushes are mathematically much rarer and beat full houses!
  • Three of a Kind Beats Straight: In standard short deck math, making three of a kind is more difficult than completing a straight.
  • Ace Can Play Low for Wheel Straight: An Ace can be used as low to make an A-6-7-8-9 straight (equivalent to A-2-3-4-5).

Short Deck Hand Rankings (Macau Hierarchy)

  1. Royal Flush: A-K-Q-J-10 of same suit
  2. Straight Flush: Five sequential cards of same suit
  3. Four of a Kind: Four matching ranks
  4. Flush: BEATS FULL HOUSE Any 5 cards of identical suit
  5. Full House: Three of a kind plus a pair
  6. Three of a Kind: BEATS STRAIGHT Three matching ranks
  7. Straight: 5 consecutive ranks (A-6-7-8-9 is the lowest straight)
  8. Two Pair: Two distinct pairs
  9. One Pair: Two matching ranks
  10. High Card: Highest individual card

♥ Omaha High Poker (4-Card) Rules

The game of massive action and drawing potential. Each player is dealt 4 private hole cards instead of 2.

The Golden Rule of Omaha

You MUST use EXACTLY TWO cards from your hand and EXACTLY THREE cards from the board. You cannot use 1 card from your hand, nor can you use 3 or 4 cards from your hand!

Example: If the board has 4 hearts and you hold only 1 heart in your hand, you DO NOT have a flush. You need at least 2 hearts in your private hole cards!

Omaha Strategy Tips

  • Connected Wraps: Four connected cards like 9-8-7-6 have up to 20 straight outs on favorable flops.
  • Double Suited Hands: Starting hands with two suits (e.g. A♠ K♠ Q♦ J♦) are the strongest starting holdings in Omaha.
  • The Nuts Matter: Because players hold 4 cards, non-nut flushes and bottom straights are vulnerable to being dominated.
