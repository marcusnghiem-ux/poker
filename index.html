♠ Royal Texas Hold'em Guide & Rules ♠

1
Royal Flush: A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠

The unbeatable hand. Ace-high straight flush of the same suit.

2
Straight Flush: 9♥ 8♥ 7♥ 6♥ 5♥

Five consecutive cards of identical suit.

3
Four of a Kind: K♠ K♥ K♦ K♣ 4♦

Four cards of matching rank plus highest kicker.

4
Full House: J♠ J♥ J♦ 8♣ 8♠

Three cards of one rank combined with a pair of another rank.

5
Flush: A♦ J♦ 9♦ 6♦ 3♦

Any five cards of the same suit, not in sequence.

6
Straight: 10♠ 9♥ 8♦ 7♣ 6♠

Five consecutive ranks of mixed suits. Ace can count high (A-K-Q-J-10) or low (5-4-3-2-A).

7
Three of a Kind: 7♠ 7♥ 7♦ K♣ 2♠

Three cards of matching rank with two unmatched kickers.

8
Two Pair: Q♠ Q♥ 9♦ 9♣ A♠

Two distinct pairs with one kicker card.

9
One Pair: 10♥ 10♦ A♠ 8♣ 4♥

Two cards of identical rank.

10
High Card: A♠ J♥ 8♦ 6♣ 2♦

No pair or sequence. Evaluated by highest single card down to fifth kicker.

1. The Dealer Button & Forced Blinds

The Dealer Button (D) rotates clockwise each hand. The player left of the dealer posts the Small Blind, and the next player posts the Big Blind. These forced bets start the action.

2. Pre-Flop

Each player is dealt 2 private hole cards. Betting begins with the player to the left of the Big Blind. Players can Fold, Call, or Raise.

3. The Flop

The dealer deals 3 community cards face-up in the center. The second round of betting takes place.

4. The Turn

A 4th community card is dealt face-up. Players evaluate their made hands and drawing outs before betting.

5. The River & Showdown

The 5th and final community card is revealed. Final betting round ensues. If two or more players remain, all reveal their hole cards in the Showdown. The best 5-card combination wins the pot!

💰 Single Main Pot & Side Pots Explained

In standard play, there is one single central pot that all active players compete for. When a player pushes All-In with fewer chips than an opponent:

  • Main Pot: Composed of the all-in player's chips matched by every active player. The all-in player is fully eligible to win this pot!
  • Side Pot: Any extra chips bet by opponents with deeper stacks above the all-in amount form a separate side pot. The all-in player cannot win the side pot.

💀 Zero Money Busted Rule

If you push all-in and lose, you drop to $0 completely. You are eliminated from the table and return to the Main Menu where you can reset your bankroll back to $1,000.
